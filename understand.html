Understand

Explore why you think and behave the way you do, and discover what may be holding you back.

Look beneath the surface.

Understanding yourself means looking beyond what you do and asking why you do it.

Ask yourself:

  • Why do I behave this way?
  • What situations trigger this behavior?
  • What beliefs influence my decisions?
  • What is holding me back?
  • What patterns do I keep repeating?
