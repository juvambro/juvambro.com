Your Juvambro Journey Step 1 of 9

Become Aware

Take a few minutes to honestly examine your current situation. There are no right or wrong answers. This is for you.

1

Where am I today?

What does my current life look like? Think about your studies, work, finances, relationships, health, habits, time and personal life.

2

What am I not satisfied with?

What would you genuinely like to improve? What situations, habits or patterns are no longer serving you?

3

What is working well?

What are your strengths? Which habits, relationships, skills or decisions are helping you?

4

What do I want to improve?

Imagine your life becoming better. Which areas would you most like to develop?

5

What is one thing I can change?

You don't need to change everything at once. What is one small action you could take today?

Awareness is your starting point.

You don't need to have everything figured out. You simply need to understand where you are before deciding where you want to go.

Don't change everything. Start with one thing.
