Step 7 of your journey

Turn intention into action.

Knowing who you want to become is important. But change happens when you begin to act like that person.

Take action.

Turn what you have discovered into small, realistic actions you can actually take.

1

What should I start doing?

What is one behaviour or habit that would move you closer to the person you want to become?

2

What is one small action I can take today?

Don't try to change everything at once. Choose one action you can realistically complete today.

3

When will I do it?

Choose a specific time or situation when you will take this action.

4

What might stop me?

Think about the obstacles, distractions or excuses that could get in your way.

5

How will I keep going?

What can you do when motivation becomes low? Think about how you can stay consistent.

Small actions create change.

You don't need to transform your life in one day. Choose one meaningful action and begin.
