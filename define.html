Decide what you want.

Awareness helps you understand where you are. Now it is time to define where you want to go.

Your Juvambro Journey Step 2 of 9

Define

Turn vague wishes into something clear. You don't need to know everything. You only need to decide what matters most right now.

1

What do I actually want?

If you could improve one important part of your life, what would you genuinely want to be different?

2

Why does this matter to me?

Why is this important? What would change in your life if you achieved it?

3

What would success look like?

Imagine that you achieved what you want. What would your life look or feel like?

4

What is my main goal right now?

Choose one goal that deserves your attention. You don't need to solve everything at once.

5

What result do I want first?

What is one meaningful result you would like to achieve from this goal?

Clarity gives direction.

You don't need to have your entire future planned. A clear direction is enough to take the next step.

Choose what matters. Then move forward.
