Think about who you can become.

Your goals describe what you want to achieve. Your identity describes the person who can achieve them.

Your Juvambro Journey Step 3 of 9

Become

Imagine the person you want to become and understand what you need to develop to move toward that future.

1

Who do I want to become?

Imagine yourself one, three or five years from now. What kind of person would you be proud to become?

2

What qualities would this person have?

Think about the qualities that would help you become the person you want to be.

3

What would this person do differently?

How would this person spend their time, make decisions and respond to difficulties?

4

What do I need to stop doing?

Which habits, behaviours or ways of thinking may be keeping you from becoming that person?

5

What do I need to start doing?

What behaviours or habits would move you closer to the person you want to become?

Becoming requires development.

You don't become a different person simply by wishing for change. You develop the knowledge, skills, habits and qualities that make your desired future possible.
